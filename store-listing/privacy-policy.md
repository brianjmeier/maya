# Maya Standup Timer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 4, 2026
**Last updated:** August 4, 2026

Maya Standup Timer is a local-only Chrome extension that places a draggable standup countdown over a browser tab selected by the user. This policy explains what information the extension handles and how it is used.

## Information stored on your device

Maya Standup Timer stores only the information needed to operate the timer:

- Countdown storage schema version, status, duration, remaining time or deadline, revision, and update timestamp
- The timer overlay's position on screen

This information is stored in `chrome.storage.local` within your Chrome profile. It stays on your device and is used only to keep the countdown working and remember where you placed the overlay.

## Information we do not collect

Maya Standup Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. It does not collect or transmit:

- Your name, email address, identifiers, or account information
- Authentication, financial, payment, or health information
- Personal communications or location
- Browsing history, visited URLs, or search history
- Website or page content
- Keystrokes, clicks, scrolling, or mouse movement as analytics

The extension does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry. It does not make network requests and does not send information to the developer or third parties.

## Permissions

Maya Standup Timer uses these Chrome permissions:

- **`activeTab`** provides temporary access to the tab only after you click the extension toolbar button.
- **`scripting`** places the packaged timer interface into that selected tab.
- **`storage`** saves timer state and overlay position locally on your device.

The extension uses access to the selected tab only to display and operate its timer interface. It does not read, copy, store, or transmit the page's content or URL.

## Remote code and external services

All executable code is included in the extension package. Maya Standup Timer does not download or execute remote code and does not use external services to provide the timer.

## Data sharing and sale

Maya Standup Timer does not share or sell information. It does not use information for advertising, creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to providing the timer.

## Retention and deletion

Timer state and overlay position remain in your Chrome profile until you clear the extension's stored data or uninstall the extension. Because this information is never transmitted to us, we cannot view, retrieve, or delete it remotely.

## Children’s privacy

Maya Standup Timer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this policy and the Chrome Web Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released. Any newly required disclosure or consent will be provided in the extension where applicable.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the verified developer contact information displayed on Maya Standup Timer's Chrome Web Store listing. No separate homepage or support URL is asserted by this policy.
